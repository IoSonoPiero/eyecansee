# Stamps

![Stamps](icon://stamp) **Stamps** (K) puts a ready-made icon on the image with a click: a check, a cross, a warning, a star and many more.

## Putting a stamp

1. Choose ![Stamps](icon://stamp) **Stamps**.
2. In the properties bar, click the icon you want.
3. Click the image. Each click puts one stamp.

## Choosing the icon

The first seven are on the bar: ![Check](icon://check) Check, ![Cross](icon://x) Cross, ![Warning](icon://triangle-exclamation-point) Warning, ![Information](icon://info) Information, ![Question](icon://circle-question-mark) Question, ![Star](icon://star) Star and ![Thumbs up](icon://thumbs-up) Thumbs up.

![More stamps](icon://ellipsis) **More stamps** opens fifty more: faces, hearts, arrows, flags, places, clocks, people, prizes, weather and others. Point at one to see its name. When the stamp in use is one of these, the button shows it.

## Options

- **Colour** (it starts green), **Size** and **Opacity**.
- Change the size in the bar, or drag a corner of the selected stamp. It stays square.

## Good to know

- A click on a stamp already placed selects it. Anywhere else on the image, a new stamp is placed; a click outside the image places none.
- To move a stamp, use ![Select](icon://mouse-pointer-2) **Select** and drag it. With Stamps, dragging moves the view.
- **I**: eyedropper. **Alt+click**: take a colour and keep the tool. **Del**: delete the selected stamp.

See also [Colours, lines and the eyedropper](editor-colors.md).
