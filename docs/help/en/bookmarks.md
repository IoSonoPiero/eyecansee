# Bookmarks

Bookmarks take you back with one click to the places you use most: a folder of this computer, a disk, a folder of a phone or of a server, or a single picture. They are handy for copying and pasting between a phone, a disk and a server.

## Adding a bookmark

Click the ![Add this folder to the bookmarks](icon://star) **star** next to the address bar: the folder open (in the viewer, the picture shown) becomes a bookmark. The status bar says so for a few seconds.

You can also use **Bookmarks > + Add current folder** in the menu bar (in the viewer **+ Add this image**).

A new bookmark takes the name of the folder or the picture; for the top of a disk or a phone, the name of the device. A place that is already a bookmark is not added twice: the star is lit, and the menu item is greyed out.

## Opening a bookmark

<!-- shot: bookmarks-menu | ritaglio | the star (lit) and the Bookmarks drop-down open under it, with three bookmarks and Configure… -->

- Click ![Bookmarks](icon://bookmark) **Bookmarks**, the drop-down just after the star, and choose a bookmark. Pointing at a bookmark shows its address.
- Or use the **Bookmarks** menu in the menu bar.
- Or press **Ctrl+Shift+D**: the drop-down opens; the **Down** arrow enters the list and **Enter** opens the bookmark chosen.

A bookmark opens exactly as if you typed its address in the address bar: a server asks for its password or the master password as usual, and a picture you turned and did not save is asked about first.

## Removing a bookmark

When the place open is a bookmark, the star is lit: click it to remove the bookmark. The status bar says so.

## Configure

**Configure…**, at the bottom of the drop-down and of the Bookmarks menu, opens the list of your bookmarks, one box each, with the name and the address under it:

- ![Change name and address](icon://pencil) changes the name and the address. **Done** or **Enter** finishes. A password typed in the address is not kept.
- ![Move up](icon://chevron-up) and ![Move down](icon://chevron-down) move the bookmark up or down in the list.
- ![Remove from the bookmarks](icon://trash) removes it.

The changes are saved when you close the window with **Close** or **Esc**.

## Good to know

- A bookmark whose place no longer exists, or whose disk or phone is not connected, is never removed by itself: choosing it says why it cannot be opened, and it stays in the list for when the device is connected again.
- A bookmark to a server folder that no longer exists opens the saved server's folder instead, or the folder where the server starts.
- Bookmarks are saved with the other settings, in `settings.json`; a server's address is kept without its password.
