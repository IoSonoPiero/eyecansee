# Selecting files

Most actions work on the files you select in the grid: copying, deleting, converting, the slideshow and more. You can select one item or many.

## One item

Click a thumbnail or a folder, or move with the arrows. The selected item has a thick border and a tinted tile. When you enter a folder nothing is selected; when you go up, the folder you came from is selected.

## Several items

- **Ctrl+click** adds an item to the selection, or takes it away. The item that was selected before is kept too.
- **Shift+click** selects everything from the last item you clicked to this one.
- **Shift+arrows** extend the selection in the same way.
- **Ctrl+A** (or **Edit > Select all**, also in the right-click menu of an empty space) selects everything in the folder.
- **Drag** with the mouse across the grid: a rectangle appears and selects the cells it touches. With **Ctrl** held, they are added to those already selected. Near the top or bottom edge the grid scrolls by itself.

A click without keys, or an arrow without Shift, goes back to a single item.

The status bar shows how many items are selected and how much their files weigh, for example "Selected: 12 (48 MB)".

## What happens to the selection

- A **right click** on one of the selected items opens a menu that works on all of them. A right click on an item outside the selection selects only that one.
- **Copy**, **Cut**, **Delete**, **Convert and resize** and **Redo thumbnails** work on all the items selected.
- **Enter** (or **Open**) opens the first of the selected files in the viewer, which then moves only among them. The **Slideshow** too shows only them (the pictures and videos among them), from the first.
- **Rename** works on one item at a time: with several selected it is greyed out.

When the selection mixes different kinds of files (for example pictures and a 3D model), an entry that works only on some of them is marked with **\*** in the menus: it acts only on those, and a line at the bottom of the menu says so.

## Two sides of the window

The arrows and Tab act on the side of the window you clicked last: the grid or the side panel. That side has a thin frame, and its selection is solid blue; on the other side the selection is paler.
