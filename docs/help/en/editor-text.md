# Text, bubbles and steps

![Text](icon://type) **Text** (T) writes words on the image, ![Speech bubble](icon://message-circle) **Speech bubble** (U) puts them in a comic-style balloon, and ![Step numbers](icon://list-ordered) **Step numbers** (N) numbers the steps of a guide.

## Writing a text

1. Choose ![Text](icon://type) **Text**.
2. Click the image where the text goes.
3. Type. The letters appear on the image itself; Enter starts a new line.
4. Press Esc, or click elsewhere, when you are done.

A text left empty goes away. While you type, the letters are shown in the interface's font; they take their own font when you are done.

To change a text already written, double-click it (with Text or Select), or select it and change it in the field of the properties bar. Undo takes back the whole change at once.

A text has no corner handles: its size is in the properties bar. To move it, use ![Select](icon://mouse-pointer-2) **Select** and drag it. With the Text tool, dragging moves the view instead.

## Options of a text

- **Colour**, **Size** and **Opacity**.
- **Outline**: a line round the letters, with its own colour and width, to read them on any photo.
- **Box**: a box behind the letters, with its colour, opacity, **Margin** and round **Corners**.

When the text is selected, the bar also has:

- **The font**: the list shows the fonts installed on the computer. Point at a name to see the text in it at once; click to choose it (the next texts use it too). Closing the list without choosing puts the font back as it was.
- **On a curve**: the text follows a line with two ends and a middle point. Drag the middle point to bend the line, the ends to stretch or turn it; the letters follow. A text on a curve is typed in the field of the bar only. With a box, the box becomes a band along the whole line.
- **Rotation**: tilts the text, from -180° to 180°.

## Speech bubbles

1. Choose ![Speech bubble](icon://message-circle) **Speech bubble**.
2. Drag to draw the bubble.
3. Type its text at once; Enter starts a new line.

The text goes to a new line at the width of the bubble, and the bubble grows taller to hold it. Its tail ends in a fifth handle: drag it to point the tail. Drag a corner to resize the bubble (Shift keeps the proportions); the text fits the new width. Double-click a bubble to change its text.

Options:

- The shape: ![Rounded](icon://message-square) rounded, ![Oval](icon://message-circle) oval, ![Square](icon://balloon-square) square, ![Thought (cloud)](icon://balloon-cloud) a cloud for thoughts (with round puffs for a tail) or ![Shout](icon://balloon-shout) spiky for a shout.
- **Border:** its colour and **Width**. **Fill:** a colour, or none. **Opacity**, for the whole bubble.
- **Letters:** their colour and size. When the bubble is selected, also their font.

## Step numbers

1. Choose ![Step numbers](icon://list-ordered) **Step numbers**.
2. Click the image: a disc with the next number appears. The next number is one more than the highest already on the image.
3. Click again for the next step.

The disc takes the colour chosen; the number is black or white, whichever reads better on it. When a step is selected, the bar has a **Number** field to change it. Change the size in the bar or by dragging a corner (the disc stays round).

A click on an existing step selects it. Anywhere else, even inside a rectangle, a new step is placed. To move a step, use Select and drag it; with Step numbers, dragging moves the view.

## Keys

- **I**: eyedropper. **Alt+click**: take a colour and keep the tool.
- **Del**: delete the selected object (outside the text field).
- **Esc**: end the text being typed, or let go of the object.
