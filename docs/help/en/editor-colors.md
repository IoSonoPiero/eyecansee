# Colours, lines and the eyedropper

The properties bar, under the toolbar, holds the style of what you draw: colour, width, opacity and the kind of line. It changes with the tool.

## Next object or selected object

The bar starts with one of two labels:

- **Next object:** nothing is selected. The style is for the next object you draw with this tool.
- **Selected object:** the changes go to the selected object, and stay for the next ones of the same kind.

EyeCanSee remembers the last style of each kind of object, also after closing. Widths are kept in proportion to the image, so a line looks the same on a small and on a large photo.

## Colours

- Eight ready-made colours: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple, black and white. The one in use is ringed.
- **Other colour**: the button after them opens a colour picker.
- Rectangles, ellipses and speech bubbles have two rows: **Border:** and **Fill:**. The fill can also be none (the white square crossed in red).
- A speech bubble also has the colour of its **Letters:**.

## Width, size and opacity

- **Width**: the thickness of lines and borders, in pixels of the image. For a magnifier, 0 means no border.
- **Size**: for texts, step numbers and stamps.
- **Opacity**: from 5% to 100%. For a spotlight the slider is called **Intensity**: how dark the rest gets.
- The highlighter and the cover box have no width: they are as large as you drag them. The cover box has no opacity: it is always solid.

## Lines

Lines, arrows, freehand strokes, rectangles and ellipses can be ![Solid](icon://line-solid) **Solid**, ![Dashed](icon://line-dashed) **Dashed** or ![Dotted](icon://line-dotted) **Dotted**.

## The eyedropper

![Eyedropper](icon://pipette) takes a colour from the image, including what is drawn on it.

1. Press **I**, or click the eyedropper in the bar.
2. Move over the image. A box by the pointer shows a magnifier with the pixels around it (the one taken is framed), the new colour over the one in use, and its hex, RGB and HSL codes.
3. Choose **1 px**, **3×3** or **5×5**: one pixel, or the average of the pixels around it.
4. Click. The colour goes to the style in the bar, its hex code goes to the clipboard, and the status bar shows its codes for a few seconds.

Esc cancels. With a drawing tool, **Alt+click** takes a colour at once and keeps the tool.

## Colours from anywhere on the screen

- **Linux**: the eyedropper button and **Shift+I** open the system's colour picker, to take a colour from anywhere on the screen. **I** takes it on the image, with the box.
- **Windows**: the eyedropper takes a colour from the image. To take one from elsewhere on the screen, press on the image and let go where the colour is; the box shows the colour under the pointer.

The eyedropper is in the bar only where there is a colour to choose: not with Select and nothing selected, not for a spotlight, a filter or a pasted part, not with Crop and rotate.
