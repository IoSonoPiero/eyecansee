# Getting started

EyeCanSee shows the pictures, videos, 3D models and CAD drawings of a folder as thumbnails, and opens them one at a time. It works in the same way on the folders of this computer, on disks and USB sticks, on Android phones and on servers (FTP and SFTP).

## The window

<!-- shot: start | panoramica | the whole window on a folder of holiday photos: menu bar, toolbar with the address bar, side panel with Devices and This computer, grid with a few folders and thumbnails (one selected), metadata panel and status bar -->

- **Menu bar** at the top: File, Edit, Image, View, Bookmarks, Tools and Help.
- **Toolbar** under it: the buttons for the most common operations, the bookmarks and the address bar. Pointing at a button shows its name and its key; on a greyed-out button it also says why it cannot be used now.
- **Side panel** on the left: your disks, phones and saved servers, and the folders of this computer. See [Folders and the side panel](folders.md).
- **Grid** in the middle: the subfolders and the files of the open folder, as thumbnails. See [The thumbnail grid](grid.md).
- **Metadata panel** under the grid: the information about the selected file. See [Image information](metadata.md).
- **Status bar** at the bottom: how many folders and files there are, and the name, size and date of the selected item.

## Opening a folder

- Click a folder in the side panel, or double-click a folder in the grid.
- Type the address of a folder in the address bar and press Enter. See [Address bar and Open location](address.md).
- Choose one of your [bookmarks](bookmarks.md).

When EyeCanSee starts, it opens the folder you were looking at when you closed it (you can turn this off in [Options](options.md)), or your home folder. If you open a picture with EyeCanSee from another program, it shows that picture.

Only one EyeCanSee runs at a time: if it is already open, a picture or folder opened again with EyeCanSee appears in the window that is already open.

## Looking at a picture

Double-click a thumbnail, or select it and press **Enter**: the picture fills the window. Press **Esc**, double-click the picture or use ![Back to the grid](icon://layout-grid) **Back to the grid** to come back. See [Viewing images](viewer.md).

## Menus, buttons and keys

- A **right click** on anything (a thumbnail, a folder, an empty space of the grid, the picture in the viewer) opens a menu with what you can do with it.
- Every action has a menu item, with its key written next to it. The list of all the keys is in [Keyboard shortcuts](keyboard.md).
- When the window is narrow, some toolbar buttons move into the ![More operations](icon://ellipsis) **More operations** menu.
- **F11** shows the window at full screen, and back.

## Help

Press **F1** at any time: this guide opens on the page about what you are doing. Press **F1** again to close it. **Help > Manual** opens the list of all the pages.
