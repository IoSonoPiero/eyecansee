# Covering parts of an image

![Cover](icon://eye-off) **Cover** (B) hides private details with a solid box: a number plate, an address, a face, a name on a document.

## Covering

1. Choose ![Cover](icon://eye-off) **Cover**.
2. Drag over what must be hidden. Hold Shift for a square.
3. The box is solid. It starts black; choose another colour in the properties bar if you like.

Drag a corner of the selected box to resize it (Shift keeps the proportions).

## What is left in the file

Nothing. In the saved file the box replaces every pixel it touches, with no soft edge, so nothing of what was under it can be read or worked back. On an Ultra HDR photo the HDR part is hidden under it too.

The box covers what you drew before it. What you draw after it stays on top.

## Cover, blur or pixelate?

The [Filters](editor-filters.md) Blur and Pixelate look like hiding, but they are not enough for private text or numbers: at a low amount they can still be read, or worked back. For those, always use Cover.

A part cut with Ctrl+X in [Selecting an area](editor-marquee.md) is covered the same way.

## Keys

- **I**: eyedropper. **Alt+click**: take a colour and keep the tool.
- **Del**: delete the selected box. **Esc**: let go of it.
