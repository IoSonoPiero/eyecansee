# Lines and arrows

![Arrow](icon://move-up-right) **Arrow** (A) and ![Line](icon://slash) **Line** (L) draw straight or bent lines, to point at something or to underline it.

## Drawing

1. Choose ![Arrow](icon://move-up-right) **Arrow** or ![Line](icon://slash) **Line**.
2. Drag on the image from where the line starts to where it ends. An arrow's head is at the end.
3. The new line is selected: its style is in the properties bar.

## Bending and moving the ends

A selected line or arrow has three handles: its two ends and its middle.

- Drag an end to move it. A bent line keeps its shape.
- Drag the middle handle to bend the line into a curve. Drop it back in the middle of the two ends and the line is straight again.
- To move the whole line, use ![Select](icon://mouse-pointer-2) **Select** and drag it.

## Options in the properties bar

- **Colour**, **Width** and **Opacity**. See [Colours, lines and the eyedropper](editor-colors.md).
- **Line**: ![Solid](icon://line-solid) solid, ![Dashed](icon://line-dashed) dashed or ![Dotted](icon://line-dotted) dotted.
- **Heads** (Arrow only): ![Filled head](icon://head-filled) filled, ![Open head](icon://head-open) open, ![Head at both ends](icon://head-double) at both ends, or ![Dot](icon://head-dot) a dot.

## Keys

- **I**: eyedropper. **Alt+click**: take a colour and keep the tool.
- **Del**: delete the selected line. **Esc**: let go of it.
