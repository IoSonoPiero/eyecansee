# Pen, highlighter and shapes

These tools draw freely by hand, mark text like a highlighter pen, and frame things with rectangles and ellipses.

## Freehand

![Freehand](icon://pencil) **Freehand** (P) follows the pointer while you drag, and smooths the stroke. The whole stroke is one object: move it with Select, and stretch it by the corners of its box.

Options: colour, width, opacity and line (solid, dashed or dotted).

## Rectangle and ellipse

![Rectangle](icon://square) **Rectangle** (R) and ![Ellipse](icon://ellipse) **Ellipse** (E): drag from one corner to the opposite one. Hold Shift for a square or a circle.

Options:

- **Border:** its colour, **Width** and line (solid, dashed or dotted).
- **Fill:** none (the white square crossed in red) or a colour.
- **Opacity**, for the whole shape.

Without a fill, the shape is picked by its border only: a click inside it goes to what is under it.

## Highlighter

![Highlighter](icon://highlighter) **Highlighter** (H): drag a box over what you want to mark. Hold Shift for a square.

It tints the photo as a real highlighter does: white becomes the colour, dark letters stay dark and readable. It starts yellow. With less **Opacity** the tint is lighter. It has no width: the box is the size you drag.

The highlighter works on the photo itself, under the objects you draw: an arrow over it keeps its own colour.

## Resizing

Drag a corner of the selected shape to resize it; the opposite corner stays where it is. Hold Shift to keep the proportions.

## Keys

- **I**: eyedropper. **Alt+click**: take a colour and keep the tool.
- **Del**: delete the selected shape. **Esc**: let go of it.

See also [Colours, lines and the eyedropper](editor-colors.md).
