# Magnifier and spotlight

![Magnifier](icon://search) **Magnifier** (Z) shows a part of the photo larger, in a round or square lens. ![Spotlight](icon://spotlight) **Spotlight** (S) darkens everything except what must stand out.

## Magnifier

1. Choose ![Magnifier](icon://search) **Magnifier**.
2. Drag on the image to draw the lens. Hold Shift for a circle or a square.
3. The lens shows the photo under it, magnified.

Options:

- ![Round](icon://circle) **Round** or ![Square](icon://square) **Square**.
- **Zoom:** 2×, 3× or 4×.
- The colour and **Width** of its border. Width 0: no border.
- **Opacity**.
- **With the objects**: the lens also shows, magnified, the objects drawn before it. Those drawn after it stay over it at their own size.

The lens always shows the filters and the cover boxes, wherever they are in the order. So it never shows what a [Cover](editor-cover.md) box hides.

## Spotlight

1. Choose ![Spotlight](icon://spotlight) **Spotlight**.
2. Drag over what must stand out. Hold Shift for a square or a circle.
3. The rest of the photo is darkened.

Options:

- ![Round](icon://circle) **Round** or ![Square](icon://square) **Square**.
- **Intensity**: how dark the rest gets.

With several spotlights, all their parts stay bright, and the rest is as dark as the darkest of them. The spotlight works on the photo: the objects you draw stay bright over it. It has no colour.

## Resizing

Drag a corner of the selected lens or spotlight to resize it. Hold Shift to keep the proportions.

## Keys

- **Del**: delete the selected object. **Esc**: let go of it.
