What the pictures of the illustrated manual (docs/help/img/*.webp) show. They are taken by
EyeCanSee itself (tools/shots.sh, the scenes in this folder) in a home folder made for them
(/home/demo), 1200 x 800 points, dark theme, English. Made up for the pictures: the servers
(example.com), the USB stick "USB STICK" and the external disk "BACKUP" (folders in memory that
the app takes for disks), and the GPS position of Grand Canyon.jpg (the South Rim of the photo's
title, approximate), added for the metadata panel.

Photos and videos (Pictures/Holiday, Videos, the USB stick, Pictures/Phone backup): the 13
photos and Bees on flowers.webm, Flowers.webm, Earth from space.webm of docs/screenshots/SOURCES.txt,
public domain or CC0 from Wikimedia Commons (authors and links there). On the USB stick and in
Phone backup they are copies renamed IMG_0001.jpg ... IMG_0108.jpg.

3D models (Documents/3D models): Avocado.glb, Lantern.glb, ToyCar.glb from the Khronos glTF
Sample Assets (https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models), CC0 1.0
as their LICENSE.md files say.

Drawings (Documents/Drawings): "United States.dxf" (usa.dxf) and "Outlines.dxf"
(hatches_2.dxf) from the examples of ezdxf (https://github.com/mozman/ezdxf), MIT licence;
"Line types.dxf", drawn for EyeCanSee with ezdxf: free to use for any purpose.
