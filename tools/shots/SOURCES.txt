What the pictures of the illustrated guide (docs/help/img/*.webp) show. They are taken by
EyeCanSee itself (tools/shots.sh, the scenes in this folder) in a home folder made for them
(/home/demo), 1200 x 800 points, dark theme, English. Made up for the pictures: the servers
(example.com), the USB stick "USB STICK" and the external disk "BACKUP" (folders in memory that
the app takes for disks). Authors, licences and sources of every file: docs/help/img/CREDITS.md.

Photos and videos (Pictures/Holiday, Videos, the USB stick, Pictures/Phone backup): the 13
photos and Bees on flowers.webm, Flowers.webm, Earth from space.webm of docs/screenshots/SOURCES.txt,
public domain or CC0 from Wikimedia Commons. In Pictures/Phone backup IMG_0001-0003.jpg are copies
of Moth, Iceberg, Sunset; on the USB stick IMG_0101-0108.jpg of Lake Sherburne, North Cascades,
Grand Canyon, Wilder Kaiser, Apple blossoms, Quebec City, Iceberg, Moth. The phone scenes copy the
same photos and Bees on flowers.webm into DCIM/EyeCanSee-guida on the phone. Lake Garda.dng (Pictures/Holiday, the metadata panel, with
the GPS position the phone wrote): the Google Pixel 6 Pro file of raw.pixls.us, CC0, in the test
files as formati/RAW/Google - Pixel 6 Pro - 16bit (4-3).dng.

3D models (Documents/3D models): Avocado.glb, Lantern.glb, ToyCar.glb from the Khronos glTF
Sample Assets (https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models), CC0 1.0
as their LICENSE.md files say.

Drawing (Documents/Drawings): Flat.dxf (this folder), a flat plan drawn for EyeCanSee with ezdxf
1.4.5, CC0 1.0; the cad scene saves it as Flat.dwg with Export drawing and
shows the DWG.
