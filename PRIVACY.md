# Privacy policy

The EyeCanSee app does not collect, store or send any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry and no advertising.

## What it connects to

- **GitHub**, only to download optional components (the video engine and FFmpeg) when you ask for them, and, in copies installed from GitHub (installer, zip, AppImage), to check for new versions (this can be turned off in Options). The Microsoft Store version does not check: the Store updates it. These requests carry what any download carries (your IP address and the app's name and version); [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) applies to them.
- **Servers you add yourself** (SFTP, FTP, FTPS), with the details you give. Saved passwords stay on your computer, encrypted if you set a master password.
- **OpenStreetMap**, only when you click "Open on the map", in your browser.

## Your files

Your images, videos, phones and drives are read and changed only on your computer and on the devices and servers you choose. Nothing is uploaded anywhere else.

## Stored on your computer

Settings, saved servers, the thumbnail cache and downloaded components, in the app's data folder. The Microsoft Store version removes them when it is uninstalled.

## This website

The website (eyecansee.montinaro.info) counts its visits with [Umami](https://umami.is) 3.4.1, running on a server of the developer, to know how many people see the site and which pages they come from. For each page opened it records the time, the page's address and title, the address of the page the visitor came from, the browser, the operating system, the kind of device (desktop, laptop, tablet or phone), the screen size, the browser's language, and the country, region and city found from the IP address. Umami does not store the IP address, sets no cookies and writes nothing on the visitor's device; the counts are not shared with anyone. Visits older than 24 months are deleted automatically every day.

The requests to umami.montinaro.info go through Cloudflare, which sees the IP address of each request.

The app itself sends no data about you or about how you use it.

## Who is responsible, and your rights

The app and the website are made by Pierluigi Montinaro, who is responsible for the data described here. The visits are counted on the basis of the developer's legitimate interest in knowing how the website is used.

You can ask what is kept about you and ask for it to be deleted. The visit counts hold no name, no IP address and no cookie, so in most cases they cannot be linked to one person. You can also lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, https://www.garanteprivacy.it).
